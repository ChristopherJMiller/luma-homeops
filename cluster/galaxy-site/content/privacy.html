Privacy

Last updated 23 September 2026

galaxy is a private home server operated by Chris Miller for family and personal use. It is not a commercial service. This page describes what it does with your information, in plain terms.

What is collected when you sign in

Signing in happens on Google's or Microsoft's own sign-in page. galaxy never receives or stores your password. What it receives back from them is:

  • your email address;
  • your name and profile picture, if your provider supplies them.

Your email address is compared against a short list of people who have been granted access. If it is not on that list, you are refused and nothing further is stored.

What is stored, and for how long

  • A session cookie in your browser, so you are not asked to sign in on every page. It expires after 7 days.
  • Server logs recording that a sign-in happened, with the email address and a timestamp. These are kept for a limited period and are used for troubleshooting and to spot unauthorised access attempts.
  • Anything you choose to upload — for example photos you add to the family archive — for as long as you or Chris leave it there.

Your email address is not stored in any database beyond the access list and those logs.

Who it is shared with

Nobody. Your information is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties. There is no advertising and no analytics or tracking on this site.

The only outside parties involved are the sign-in provider you choose (Google or Microsoft), who handle the authentication itself under their own privacy policies.

Where it lives

On hardware in Chris's home. Backups of the data held here are stored, encrypted in transit and at rest, with a cloud storage provider (Backblaze B2) located in the United States.

Your choices

  • You can stop using galaxy at any time; sign out and the session cookie is discarded.
  • You can ask for your access to be removed, and for content you contributed to be deleted, by emailing the address below.
  • You can ask what is held about you, and it will be told to you.

Children

galaxy is not directed at children and no account is created for anyone without an invitation from Chris.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.
