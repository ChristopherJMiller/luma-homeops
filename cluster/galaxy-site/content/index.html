galaxy

A private home server

galaxy is a small server run by Chris Miller at home. It hosts family photos and videos, media libraries, and a handful of personal tools. It is not a product, and there is nothing here to sign up for.

Signing in

Access is by invitation. If you have been given a link to something on chrismiller.xyz, you sign in with an account you already have — Google or Microsoft — and galaxy checks your email address against a short list of people who are allowed in.

galaxy never sees your password. Signing in happens on Google's or Microsoft's own page. All galaxy receives back is your email address and name.

Who to contact

Questions about your access, or about data held here, go to chris@chrismiller.xyz.
